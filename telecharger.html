Setup

Three steps, ten minutes

On the server PC

Run the server MSI (Windows asks for permission), then click the notification to open the web interface: enter the band name and your nickname, then "Create the group". The invite link and the group password are shown.

On the other PCs

Open the invite link, "Install on this PC", choose a nickname, "Let's go". The 28JoursBox folder appears on the desktop.

On phones

Open the invite link (or scan the QR code), enter a nickname, then "Add to Home Screen". That's it.

💾 How much storage?

28JoursBox itself adds little: about 45 MB per analyzed song for the separated tracks (measured: 154 songs = 6.9 GB). What fills the disk is your own files: rehearsal WAVs and Cubase / Studio One projects. Edited and deleted files are also kept 30 days (versions and trash).

Your filesSeparated tracksTotal
100 songs in MP3 / m4a (about 5 MB each)0.5 GB4.5 GB≈ 5 GB
100 rehearsals in WAV, 4 min (about 40 MB each)4 GB4.5 GB≈ 9 GB
20 multitrack Cubase / Studio One projects (1 to 5 GB each)20–100 GB—≈ 20–100 GB

Tracks are separated automatically overnight for m4a files, and on request for WAV and MP3. Plan an external disk of the same size as your data for backups.

⏱️ How long does analysis take?

Each new song is prepared overnight (1 am to 7 am): beats, chords, separated tracks and fine analysis. Measured on an 8-core PC: about 2.6 minutes of processing per minute of music, so about 10 minutes for a 4-minute song and around 30 songs per night. On a 4-core PC, count about twice as long. New songs are ready the next morning; a large first import can take a few nights. Heavy processing is capped (at most 3 cores for analysis, 6 for separation, one song at a time) so the server stays responsive.

🖥️ Members' PCs

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 320 MB for the software, plus the space of the folders you choose to keep on this PC. Cubase or Studio One optional: project locks are set automatically if they're installed.

📱 Phones

iPhone or Android with a recent browser (Safari, Chrome). Nothing to install, no storage used: songs play from the server. On mobile data, listening uses about 0.2 to 0.3 Mbit/s.
