Setup

Three steps, ten minutes

On the server PC

Run the server MSI, click the small link "This PC is the band's server", enter the band name and your nickname. 28JoursBox shows the invite link.

On the other PCs

Open the invite link, "Install on this PC", choose a nickname, "Let's go". The 28JoursBox folder appears on the desktop.

On phones

Open the invite link (or scan the QR code), enter a nickname, then "Add to Home Screen". That's it.

Requirements

What you need

💻 Server PC

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, ideally 4 cores or more. Disk space for your songs. It stays on: turn off sleep when plugged in.

🖥️ Members' PCs

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Cubase or Studio One optional: project locks are set automatically if they're installed.

📱 Phones

iPhone or Android with a recent browser (Safari, Chrome). Nothing to install. Works on mobile data thanks to Tailscale, set up from the server wizard.
